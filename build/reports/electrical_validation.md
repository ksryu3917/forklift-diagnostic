## Accuracy gates
- Every executable electrical circuit must have an assistant-redrawn simplified diagram with measurement points.
- OEM full-sheet/crops are secondary evidence, not the primary diagnostic UI.
- Static continuity alone does not clear a circuit; loaded voltage-drop/backprobe/dynamic reproduction is used where applicable.
- Exact fuse cavity / connector numeric pin roles remain UNKNOWN/OEM VERIFY unless the source resolves them.
- 60Ω CAN termination is not treated as a model-specific criterion until this forklift network topology/terminators are OEM-verified.
